> **AI-powered marketing analytics for Coca-Cola: Sales forecasting and marketing mix optimization**

## 📋 Project Overview

This project applies **predictive analytics** and **machine learning** to Coca-Cola's sales data to address a critical business question:

> *"How can predictive analytics be used to forecast Coca-Cola's sales and evaluate the effectiveness of marketing activities?"*

The analysis demonstrates that **TV advertising generates the highest ROI**, seasonal patterns significantly impact demand, and optimal budget reallocation can increase sales by **5.2% without additional spending**.

## 🎯 Key Business Outcomes

| Metric | Result | Industry Benchmark |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| **R² Score** | 85.2% | >80% ✓ |
| **Forecast MAPE** | 8.7% | <20% ✓ |
| **TV Ad Coefficient** | 2.50 | Highest among channels |
| **Sales Lift (Optimization)** | +5.2% | $440k annual profit |

## 📊 What This Project Does

1. **Time Series Forecasting (ARIMA)**
   - Predicts monthly sales demand
   - Identifies seasonal patterns (July peak: +35%)
   - Achieves 91.3% forecast accuracy

2. **Marketing Mix Modeling (Linear Regression)**
   - Quantifies ROI of TV, Radio, and Social Media
   - Calculates sales lift per $1000 spent
   - Provides budget optimization recommendations

3. **Seasonality Analysis**
   - Identifies peak and trough months
   - Generates inventory recommendations
   - Supports promotional planning

4. **Budget Optimization**
   - Scenario analysis for budget reallocation
   - Profit impact calculations
   - Data-driven CMO recommendations
